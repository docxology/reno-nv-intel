2     Abstract
Regional civic software must travel without carrying another city’s facts or eligibility assump-
tions. This study implements Reno, Nevada as a thin consumer of shared regional methods,
with local ownership of source selection, geographic policy, retained evidence and manuscript
prose. The case exposes a provider boundary: Reno’s Municode collections were not retrievable
in initial public access probes, while the City’s oﬀicial Title 18 PDF was accessible. We there-
fore preserve a separately identified document edition rather than inventing a municipal-code
scraper identity.
The retained edition contains 555 physical PDF pages and 21850 nonempty extracted lines. Ev-
ery physical page, including front matter and any empty pages, contributes to the denomina-
tor. The public deployment declares 23 sources and enables 5 national-provider monitors with
Reno-specific parameters. These are configuration counts, not successful-collection counts. Re-
production rehashes the original PDF and replays page extraction before deriving the full de-
scriptive profile, search index and manuscript evidence. Native PDF and offline HTML use
the actual pinned docxology/template API. The contribution is an inspectable composition and
publication method; it does not establish complete legal coverage, current law, hazard absence,
model factuality or civic benefit.


3     Introduction
3.1   A regional system must preserve its evidence boundary
A region is more than a label on a dashboard. Its jurisdiction, source publisher, document edi-
tions, forecast zones and public-service providers determine which operations are meaningful.
Reusing software from Crescent City or Davis is appropriate; reusing their municipal corpus,
coastal alerts or California-specific eligibility as Reno evidence is not. A thin consumer needs
enough local ownership to make these differences explicit while leaving acquisition, validation,
search, figures and rendering in one shared implementation.
Reno provides a useful engineering case because its publicly linked code system differs from
the eCode360 provider used by the earlier consumers. The City Attorney’s guidance identifies
the Clerk’s role in publishing the municipal code [City of Reno, 2026b]. This project follows the
City’s source identity but does not infer that one access mechanism works because another does.
Initial access errors for the two Municode collections are recorded as an availability boundary.
The oﬀicial Title 18 PDF supplies an accessible, explicitly bounded document edition [City of
Reno, 2026a]. The study asks whether this edition can be acquired, inspected, searched and
published without claiming that it is the whole Reno municipal code.

3.2   Relation to data and reproducibility scholarship
PROV-DM separates data entities, producing activities and responsible agents [World Wide
Web Consortium, 2013]. Here, the PDF is an entity, extraction is an activity and local source
ownership remains distinct from shared software ownership. The receipts implement those
distinctions pragmatically; they are not a complete PROV serialization or a conformance claim.
W3C guidance treats provenance, quality information, versioning and preservation as publi-
cation concerns [World Wide Web Consortium, 2017]. The design retains both the publisher’s
stated edition and the acquisition time, so later rendering cannot silently advance the source
clock. It also distinguishes HTTP reachability from semantic acceptance: a successful response
does not prove complete or current content.
The FAIR principles motivate discoverability, interpretable metadata and reusable identifiers

[Wilkinson et al., 2016]. The project publishes source identities and machine-readable commit-
ments but does not claim a formal FAIR assessment. Dataset documentation provides another
useful discipline: intended use, composition and collection conditions should travel with data
[Gebru et al., 2021]. Data statements additionally emphasize the limits of transferring language
data between contexts [Bender and Friedman, 2018]. This municipal administrative text is nei-
ther a representative sample of residents nor evidence of their preferences.
The reported contribution has three parts: a provider-independent document plane in region-
intel; an independent Reno deployment that composes that plane with national monitors; and
a complete template-rendered review packet whose counts and figures derive from retained
bytes. No social, causal or model-performance result is asserted.


4     Methods
4.1   Ownership and configuration
The Reno repository contains configuration, public source evidence, manuscript sources, ac-
ceptance tests, workflows and a small importer. Shared methods are imported from the full
commit eed4cdb9e814a6cfc12ecb961611996378672bbf. The deployment identity is reno-nv. Its
geographic anchor and rectangular watch extent are engineering parameters around Reno; they
are not surveyed city boundaries. Washoe County FIPS 32031 selects county-level drought ob-
servations, which must not be described as a city-only measurement. The NWS policy uses
public forecast zone NVZ003, whose coverage includes areas beyond Reno.
The enabled roster consists of earthquake, weather, air quality, drought and smoke. Declaring
a monitor permits execution; it does not certify its response. The required compatibility policy
document also contains fields for disabled adapters. Those fields confer no runtime eligibil-
ity. California wildfire, road, utility and fuel adapters are not enabled for Reno. Nevada 511,
City road closures, flood management, emergency management and public-health links remain
separate source-catalog entries where no corresponding verified local adapter is selected.

4.2   PDF acquisition and replay
The document descriptor binds a stable document ID to an exact configured source ID and
URL, a human-readable title, the stated edition and rights information. The captured PDF is
limited to 16 MB, the collection to 64 MB, and extraction to 10,000 physical pages per document.
Live acquisition uses a bounded public HTTPS transport and preserves HTTP failures. In this
deployment the shared HTTP client returned 403 for the City PDF, while a separate bounded,
unauthenticated curl capture succeeded. We therefore use an explicit recorded-capture import.
Its source ID, URL, original retrieval time, declared transport, status and PDF hash must match
the deployment and admitted bytes. The transport declaration is operator-recorded provenance
backed by the actual command evidence; the import is not a retrospective live-adapter pass.
Poppler pdftotext runs with layout preservation and explicit UTF-8 output. Its terminal form-
feed boundary defines physical pages; page numbers are one-based and include the cover and
contents.
For every page the receipt retains text, its SHA-256 and the number of nonempty extracted lines.
It also retains the PDF hash, byte size, acquisition timestamp and extractor version. A complete
collection is promoted transactionally; a failed acquisition preserves the previous accepted col-
lection and leaves diagnostic staging. On replay, the original PDF is rehashed and extracted
again. Exact page membership and text must agree. Edited extracted text does not pass merely
because its own hash was recomputed.

Line counts are properties of a particular extraction and layout. They do not identify legal provi-

OCR, statutory interpretation or completeness test is inferred from this path.

The shared renderer derives numerical variables, a full page-level CSV, descriptive statistics and
publication figures from captured inputs. It fingerprints the deployment, policy, source bytes,
lockfile, prose, cover and renderer implementation. Claim-ledger evidence must name captured
public files. Native rendering calls the pinned template’s RenderManager for combined PDF

evidence and does not supply independent scientific approval.

5.2   Composition and source availability
The source catalog contains 23 explicitly selected entries. The monitor roster contains 5 enabled
adapters. Source reachability receipts, when retained, report each check’s original clock and
HTTP result. They do not turn a reachable landing page into a parsed operational feed. Initial
public probes of the Municode administrative and land-development collections returned ac-
cess errors. The shared HTTP client also returned 403 for Title 18; its retained bytes came from
an explicit import of the separately recorded bounded curl capture. The Title 18 document is
therefore published as a separate document collection, with no eCode360 identity and no claim
of full municipal-code capture.
The live dashboard, retained observations and native manuscript are distinct artifacts. Rebuild-
ing the static site can reproduce the document search and review packet without a new network
acquisition. A separately run collection may discover later observations, including unavailable
states; it does not revise this manuscript’s retained document clock until a deliberate new evi-
dence build occurs.


6     Discussion
6.1   Composability requires typed limits
The useful generalization is a shared method that preserves source-specific meaning. A PDF
collection and a structured eCode360 corpus have different units and different validation oppor-
tunities. Treating one as the other would produce superficially uniform counts while obscuring
a scientific limitation. This implementation instead shares bounded transport, filesystem cus-

                                               6
tody, transaction semantics, publication correspondence and native rendering while making
document units explicit.
This distinction also clarifies thin orchestration. Reno owns the source selection and policy;
it does not copy a scraper or a renderer. Provider support evolves in the core, while a full
immutable dependency pin lets an existing consumer retain an already qualified behavior. The
manuscript and static site are projections of retained local inputs rather than independently
edited summaries. This reduces one class of disagreement between prose, figures and public
downloads. It does not remove the need to inspect legal sources or assess interface usefulness.
Reproducible computational research emphasizes preserving the information required to re-
cover results [Sandve et al., 2013]. For this case, the important recovery target is the descriptive
extraction and its publication artifacts. Native tool versions, original PDF bytes, source clocks
and the full page denominator matter more than a generic success badge. A machine that lacks
Poppler or the pinned template must fail with a visible prerequisite rather than substitute a
different rendering or extraction method silently.

6.2   Absence and operational interpretation
A source-catalog entry is an invitation to inspect an authoritative source, not evidence that its
entire archive was parsed. A meeting portal may load a JavaScript application while yielding
no static agenda links. An air-quality station may be unavailable or too distant for a selected
radius. A drought observation is county-level and has its own publication cadence. None of
these states establishes that a service is quiet or a hazard absent.
The implementation supports human checking by preserving unavailable states and linking
to original publishers. It does not promise emergency delivery, exhaustive local reporting or
legal advice. Future usability studies should observe whether readers understand the difference
between a retained edition, a reachable source and a live monitor result. Such studies require a
protocol, participant protections and their own evidence; no reader outcome is supplied here.


7     Limitations, Ethics, and Threats to Validity
7.1   Data statement
The document plane contains one oﬀicial Reno Title 18 PDF edition, including its front matter,
tables and illustrations. The administrative code, later ordinances, other titles, archived editions
and county regulations are not implied members. Washoe County is a separate jurisdiction even
where it provides regional services. The source catalog is a curated engineering selection, not a
complete census of public information.
The acquisition records technical provenance, not legal authority. A publisher may update a file
at the same URL, and later amendments may exist elsewhere. The stated cover date is reported
as such. It is not a statement that the retained text includes every amendment as of acquisition.
A preflight qpdf check of the publisher PDF returned a stream warning (exit 3); the original
bytes are preserved without repair. Exact extraction replay does not resolve that warning or
verify every source illustration. Text extraction also does not reproduce visual reading order
perfectly. Users should consult the original PDF and responsible public oﬀices for legal deci-
sions.

7.2   Rights, privacy and automated assistance
The project preserves attribution and source identity. Its repository license covers original
project contributions and does not grant rights over third-party illustrations, marks, linked


                                                 7
websites or incorporated material. The cover is original generated conceptual artwork, cred-
ited separately and not presented as a map or photograph. Analytical figures are deterministic
transformations of retained page counts. No resident-level profile, private record, hidden iden-
tifier or access-control bypass is part of this study.
NIST’s AI risk framework treats evaluation and context as ongoing responsibilities [National
Institute of Standards and Technology, 2023]. This study supplies no empirical model-answer
evaluation and no NIST certification. Shared language-model features, if later configured, need
their own retrieval, citation and factuality qualification. The public static search is lexical page
search, not a generative answer. A valid hash cannot establish truth, fairness, legal correctness
or an acceptable social use.

7.3   Preparation and responsibility
Software and manuscript preparation used AI assistance, including the separately credited con-
ceptual cover. The named author must review scientific, legal and publication claims before a
formal release. Automated checks and independent technical artifact review do not supply
domain-expert approval.

7.4   Unestablished outcomes
No civic benefit, hazard reduction, regulatory burden, legal completeness, accessibility certifica-
tion, legal interpretation or formal release approval is established. Rendered-output inspection
checks visible artifacts within a stated environment. It does not establish PDF/UA conformance
or replace review by domain experts. The packet is a review draft intended to make further re-
view possible.

To create a new native edition, configure the actual pinned template checkout and its frozen
rendering environment, install the documented native tools, then run:

The REGION_TEMPLATE_ROOT and REGION_TEMPLATE_PYTHON environment vari-

8.2   New observations and version changes
bun run documents:acquire
bun run sources:check
bun run gov-meetings
bun run alerts
bun run observations:seed

Where a public source is unavailable to the shared client, preserve the failed acquisition. A
separately recorded public capture can be admitted explicitly:

bun run documents:import -- --source output/document-capture

The import directory and strict capture.json schema are documented in the shared document
methods. Never invent successful transport metadata or advance a captured source clock dur-
ing import.
These commands make new observations and therefore change evidence. They are not equiva-
lent to replaying the retained edition. Check source rights and profile policy before collection;
inspect unavailable states afterward. A changed seed, configuration, lockfile, prose or renderer
requires new input commitments and a new rendered packet. Exact Git publication checks
compare the public working bytes against HEAD or the staged index; they neither stage files
nor publish by themselves.

8.3   Validation scope
Meaningful negative controls alter the input that a check is supposed to protect: change original
PDF bytes, rehash edited page text, remove a page, change a source URL, exchange a profile,
add an unexpected published member or modify a tracked native asset. Such cases must fail.
Broad core qualification, consumer acceptance, exact-byte Pages replay, hosted workflow results
and visible artifact review remain separately reportable evidence. A failure in one lane is not
erased by a pass in another.


9     Conclusion
Reno can be deployed as a thin regional consumer while preserving a meaningful provider
boundary. The shared document method retains the oﬀicial Title 18 PDF, replays all physical
pages, derives a complete descriptive extraction profile and publishes search and native review
artifacts from the same evidence. Local identity and selected national monitors remain explicit
configuration. The design makes the accessible document useful without representing it as a
complete current municipal code.

                                               9
The resulting review packet supports technical inspection and further scholarly review. Contin-
uing legal currency, interpretive validity, accessibility, model-answer quality and public useful-
ness require additional evidence. Future additions should enter through shared, tested meth-
ods and explicitly admitted local policy rather than copied implementations or inherited obser-
vations.
